# BinGo demo video script (90-120 s)
Narration: AI voice (e.g. Amazon Polly). Screen: real live app. AI video only for the intro.

1. 0:00-0:12 Hook: 1990s one clean waste spot vs. today's scattered trash.
2. 0:12-0:25 Problem: school runs, night shifts, vacations, furniture and event waste.
3. 0:25-0:40 Solution (introduce the name BinGo): a designated zone, five vehicles (wet, sanitary, dry, furniture, event), one ID per household.
4. 0:40-1:00 Resident scans the QR on the wet-waste vehicle, gets a green tick and a BinGo card square fills.
5. 1:00-1:15 Government dashboard: same tick appears; a vehicle hits 75%, swap alert, replacement arrives.
6. 1:15-1:30 Out-of-station toggle, and the opt-in wellness check-in (ask resident first, then contact).
7. 1:30-1:45 Architecture slide + impact numbers (label simulated data).
